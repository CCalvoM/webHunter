# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Spanish freelancers and small digital agencies (1–5 people) who sell web development services to local businesses. They prospect actively: searching cities by sector, identifying businesses with no or poor web presence, and sending personalised pitches. They work fast, context-switch constantly, and need a tool that keeps up with them — not one they have to manage.

## Product Purpose

Cloza closes the full outreach loop for web freelancers: find businesses without a web presence, audit their digital footprint with AI, generate a personalised sales pitch, and track every lead through a Kanban pipeline to close. Success means a freelancer can go from cold search to sent pitch in under 5 minutes, and never lose track of a follow-up.

## Brand Personality

Ágil, inteligente, cercano. The tool of a sharp professional who knows their craft — not a corporate platform, not a startup toy. It respects the user's time, surfaces what matters, and stays out of the way.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (HubSpot, Salesforce): corporate blue/orange, metric overload, every action buried three levels deep.
- Startup hype aesthetic: gradient backgrounds, glassmorphism cards, decorative blur effects, hero metrics with glow.
- Heavy Material Design: exaggerated shadows, elevation for its own sake, outdated Google aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over ceremony.** Every interaction should feel instant. No loading spinners where skeletons suffice, no confirmation modals for reversible actions.
2. **Signal over noise.** Show the user what they need to act on now. Empty states are prompts, not apologies.
3. **Earn every element.** If a UI element doesn't help the user find, pitch, or close a lead, it doesn't belong on the screen.
4. **Consistent without being boring.** The design system should be tight enough to feel coherent across all surfaces, loose enough to let important moments breathe.
5. **Proche, pas familier.** Warm and direct in tone, precise in layout. Like a well-organised desk, not a friendly poster.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast.
- Reduced motion support required (pipeline drag-and-drop, audit generation loader, toast animations).
- Spanish-language interface throughout. No mixed-language UI strings.
